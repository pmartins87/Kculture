## Frozen absolute compatibility rule

A candidate is an eligible population survivor only if all are true:

- every edge has 12 valid games and zero errors/non-DONE;
- mean edge score >= 0.45;
- at least 8/11 edges score >= 0.25;
- at most 3 zero-score edges;

This is deliberately a broad safety/coverage requirement, not a claim of top-10 strength.
