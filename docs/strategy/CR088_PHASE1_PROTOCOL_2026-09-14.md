## Frozen factorial population

Each of the seven bases produces six deterministic members:

1. unchanged base;
2. CR086 latent-supply ordering of existing premium SELLs;
3. bounded risk SELL cap 8, price >= 1.00x item base price;
4. bounded risk SELL cap 8, price >= 1.25x item base price;
5. bounded risk SELL cap 16, price >= 1.00x item base price;
6. bounded risk SELL cap 16, price >= 1.25x item base price.

Risk SELL variants use only own current inventory, public prices/market state,
public farms/town and the independently confirmed legal CR086 upper estimate
of opponent latent supply.  They add at most one premium-product sale per step,
never touch WHEAT/feed, never remove a base market order, never modify physical
actions and cap the final queue at the engine limit of ten.
