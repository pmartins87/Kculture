## Route rigidity is dominant

- 10/20 teams had **100% identical full actions for all first 144 turns**;
- 11/20 had 100% identical physical actions for all first 144 turns;
- mean first-144 full-action modal agreement: **94.51%**;
- median first-144 full-action modal agreement: **99.77%**;
- mean first-144 market-action agreement: **98.41%**.

Rank 1 was exactly identical through the first 72 turns across its three games, then showed only 21 variable turns by turn 144; its market agreement through 144 was 98.38%.

Rank 2 was more state-responsive, but market behavior was still 96.06% modal-agreement through turn 144.

Interpretation: current elite play is still primarily a strong route/replay backbone with sparse state response, not a broadly reactive online policy.
